Detailed Description

OCP.1 TCP client with automatic reconnection.

Inherits socket I/O from Ocp1Connection and reconnect timing from NanoTimer. When start() is called, a timer fires periodically and attempts connectToSocket() until it succeeds. Once connected, connectionMade() calls onConnectionEstablished. On disconnect (detected by the read thread), connectionLost() calls onConnectionLost and the timer resumes retrying.
