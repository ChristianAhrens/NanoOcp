Detailed Description

A fully-qualified DS100 remote parameter: identifier, address, value.

Callers receive populated RemoteObjects via onRemoteObjectReceived, and send them (with the desired value in Var) via setObjectValue().

enum NanoOcp1::SoundscapeController::RemoteObject::RemObjIdent

Enumerates every controllable or observable DS100 parameter.

Naming mirrors the OCA object hierarchy on the device. X/Y/XY split variants (Positioning_SourcePosition_X, _Y, _XY and the CoordinateMapping equivalents) and Scene_Previous/Next/Recall are Umsci-level conveniences mapped to the underlying XYZ or SceneAgent OCA objects at the protocol level.
